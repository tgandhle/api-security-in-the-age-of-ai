What the course does not cover

  • The entries marked above as not covered or partly covered.
  • GraphQL, gRPC and WebSocket beyond module 33. That module shows where the controls of earlier modules stop applying under each protocol. It leaves out GraphQL subscriptions, gRPC-Web, attacks on HTTP/2 itself, and injection through these protocols as a subject of its own.
  • SAML. No lesson teaches it. Module 35 teaches OpenID Connect.
  • Testing tools. Module 34 teaches a method for testing an API's security and what a clean result proves. It recommends no scanner or other tool.
